Study Frog

Offline creative study workspace

Creative studio

Build study graphics from imported photos, labels, and simple marks, then export a real PNG. This page does not generate AI images. Image, video, and text-reading tools run only when you connect a computer you control.

Canvas marks

PNG export saves the canvas pixels, including photos, labels, and marks. The alignment grid is only a guide. Undo keeps the last 20 canvas states. Ctrl+Z or Command+Z undoes a mark when you are not typing in a field.

Saved projects

Projects stay in this browser. Download a JSON backup before clearing site data. Browser storage is limited and is not a long-term archive.

Science Explorer

Ten linked offline schematics cover body organization and mitosis. They are simplified teaching drawings with OpenStax references, not medical or diagnostic images.
